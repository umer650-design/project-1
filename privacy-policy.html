Privacy Policy

Information You Provide: We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, submit an application, request services, or contact us for support. This includes:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Professional credentials, certifications, and licenses
  • Employment history, work experience, and references
  • Resume, cover letter, and application materials
  • Background check authorization and results
  • Company name and business contact information (for clients)
  • Payment and billing information

Sensitive Personal Information. The resume and certifications you upload may contain sensitive personal information. We ask that you do not include SSNs or other government identifiers in your application. We use any sensitive personal information you do provide only for the purposes you requested.

Information Collected Automatically. Our web host may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and operational purposes for a limited period.

How We Use Information

  • Staffing Services: To process applications, verify qualifications, conduct background checks, match candidates with job opportunities
  • Client Services: To fulfill service requests, manage client relationships, and provide solutions
  • Communications: To send job opportunities, respond to inquiries, provide customer support, and send service-related notifications
  • Compliance: To verify credentials, conduct required screenings, and comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Business Operations: To analyze usage trends, improve our services, prevent fraud, and maintain security
  • Marketing: To send promotional communications about our services (you may opt out at any time)

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Clients: When you apply for or are matched with a position, we share relevant professional information with the hiring client. We obtain your consent before sharing your information for specific opportunities.
  • Service Providers: We share information with third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf under written contracts that prohibit them from using your information for any other purpose.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, court order, or government request.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity.

Data Retention

  • Candidate applications: up to 24 months from the date of submission, after which we delete or anonymize the data
  • Business inquiries: up to 12 months from the date of submission
  • Placed workers: employment and placement records are retained for 7 years after the end of your assignment
  • Background checks: results are retained in accordance with applicable laws
  • Client business contact information: retained for the duration of the client relationship and for 7 years thereafter

You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

Data Security

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Incident response procedures

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Portability: Request a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or contacting us
  • Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. We will respond to your request within 45 days.

California Privacy Rights

  • Right to Know: Request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing: We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information: You may request that we limit our use of sensitive personal information to what is necessary to perform the services you requested
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not deny services or provide a different level of service because you exercised your privacy rights

Global Privacy Control (GPC): We honor the Global Privacy Control browser signal as a valid opt-out of any future sale or sharing of your personal information.

How to Submit a Request. You may submit a request to know, delete, correct, or limit the use of your personal information by emailing us at info@finsouls.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line, or by writing to us at the address below.

Third-Party Services

  • Transactional Email: to deliver form submissions to our team's inbox
  • Cloud Service Providers: to host our website and process form submissions
  • Background Check Providers: to conduct employment background checks and verify credentials
  • Payment Processors: to process payments and billing

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We do not currently use cookies, analytics trackers, or advertising pixels on this website.

Local browser storage. We use your browser's localStorage to temporarily save your progress while you are filling out forms. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us until you submit the form.

If we enable analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy and provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link and honor opt-out requests.

Global Privacy Control (GPC). We honor the GPC browser signal as a valid opt-out of any future sale or sharing of your personal information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

Changes to This Policy

  • Notify you by email (if you have provided one) or by posting a prominent notice on our website
  • Obtain your consent where required by law

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of our services after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
